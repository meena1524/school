M.M.Bright Future Sr.Sec.School, Gorakhpur

Functioning under the aegis of M.M.Bright Future Sr.Sec.School, Gorakhpur, M.M.Bright Future Sr.Sec.School, situated on NH-9 between two historical cities, Hisar and Bhuna, is one of the most prestigious H.B.S.E. Affiliated Schools of the region. Endowed with State Of The Art Infrastructure, Lush Green Premises and World Class Facilities, the school has become a rendezvous of more than 1200 aspiring learners from the vicinity.

Our well qualified, erudite and highly committed teaching faculty is religiously devoted towards performing a wide array of duties and responsibilities to justify the task entrusted upon them. With a perfect blend of theoretical and practical knowledge imparted in smart classrooms, they make studies a pleasant and interesting experience for the knowledge seekers.
