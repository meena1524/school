Procedure for Registration

The new academic session commences in early April every year. Admission is open to Nursery, Primary, Middle and Secondary classes for all, subject to the availability of seats, without any discrimination on the basis of caste, creed, color, sex, religious denomination & nationality. The admission process comprises Registration of the Aspirant and an Interactive Session (Aptitude or Performance Test) conducted on the parameters determined by School Admission Committee. The admission of a child will guarantee a complete acceptance by the parent/guardian of all school norms which are in force at present or as amended from time to time on the discretion of the school.

Eligibility

The school reserves all the rights to admission and its decision whatsoever cannot be questioned. 25% seats are reserved for Economically Weaker Section as per R.T.E. Procedure for Registration Prospectus along with Registration Form is available at the School Reception Counter against a payment of Rs. 1000 in cash (Rs. 800 for prospectus + Rs. 200 for registration) during working hours on any working day.

The registration fee is neither refundable nor adjustable. Post registration, the applicant is required to appear for the Interactive Session (Aptitude or Performance Test) the result of which will pave the way further for the admission.

Requirement of Documents at the Time of Admission

  1. Duly Filled Registration Form (Approved By Admission Committee)
  2. Date of Birth Certificate: Self attested photocopy of the applicant's Date of Birth Certificate issued by the Registrar of Birth & Death. Once submitted, no request for change in Date of Birth shall be entertained by the school in any circumstances. Any falsification in this regard will lead to cancellation of admission forthwith.
  3. Transfer Certificate (Original)
  4. Mark Sheet or Report Card of the Class Last Attended (Attested Photocopy)
  5. Recent Passport Size Photographs (2 Each of Mother, Father & Any Other Guardian and 6 of Applicant)
  6. Migration Certificate: This is required for admission in XI Class in case of the applicants from any state board other than C.B.S.E.
  7. Attested Photocopy of X Class Result for Admission in XI Class
  8. Photocopy of Aadhar Card (Applicant's & Parents')
  9. Email ID, Contact No.(s) and Correspondence Address on a Separate Plain Paper (Self Attested)
