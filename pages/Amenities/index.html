Science Lab

Laboratories are known to be stimulating places for the young minds where they can explore, probe and experiment to unravel the truths behind the facts of life and generate new facts as well. In order to cater to the needs of enthusiastic scientists in making, the school possesses well-designed, spacious and well-equipped Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics and Social Sciences Laboratories. They have all sets of accurate digital measuring instruments, kits, experimental gadgets, models, charts and other necessary apparatus. Under the assistance of efficient teachers and lab-assistants, the students here can perform a wide array of experiments and strengthen their scientific, investigative, reasoning and social skills.
